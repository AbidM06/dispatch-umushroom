## Consequences for the 07:45 job

- Any price that cannot be fetched is published as `unverified` with the Friday close, never left blank or guessed.
- For oil and credit spread the job has no reliable cloud source. Either run the note from the Mac (where the Dispatch already holds free Yahoo and FRED data) or accept those two lines as `unverified` web-search figures.
