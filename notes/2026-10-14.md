## What happened and why
- **Korea:** Tuesday's fall had several causes in the reports: China's CXMT planning new server memory products, a Financial Times report on OpenAI's revenue, and heavy foreign selling. Foreign investors sold a net KRW 4.5 trillion on Tuesday (Korea Times and Asia Business Daily agree). SK Hynix closed down 10.09% at KRW 1,443,000 and Samsung down 7.39% at KRW 232,000. On Wednesday both rebounded about 2-3% early, foreigners flipped between small net buying and net selling, and the gain narrowed through the session. The five-session slide in the index was close to 14%.
- **LKOR:** The fund fell 3.70% on Xetra on Tuesday to EUR 165.74, less than the KOSPI's 5.46%. The 13 Oct note estimated EUR 162.7 by arithmetic; the observed price is higher.
- **US:** Chips were mixed. TSM rose 0.17% and NVDA 0.97%. VST rose 2.68% and CEG 0.65%. EWZ fell 1.54%. A headline in one article says the Dow lost about 170 points; I did not verify it.
- **IREN:** It opened at 33.31, touched 32.69 and recovered to close at 34.80, exactly where it closed on Monday. Alpha Vantage and StockAnalysis both show that close, and a 35.07 after-hours quote is consistent with it. Neither IREN nor Anthropic has confirmed the reported contract.
- **Rates and the Fed:** The 10-year yield eased about 3bp on Tuesday. One source puts the chance of a hold on 28 Oct near 77% (so about 23% for a hike) and an 88% chance of a hike by December. New York Fed President Williams said one more hike may be appropriate this year.
